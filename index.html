Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does it take to build a website?

Depending on the complexity, a standard website takes 2–4 weeks. More complex web applications may take 6–8 weeks. We always provide a clear timeline before starting.

What is your pricing model?

We offer competitive, project-based pricing. Every project starts with a free consultation where we understand your needs and provide a transparent quote with no hidden fees.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Absolutely! We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages to ensure your website stays updated, secure, and running smoothly at all times.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes! We specialize in website redesigns. We'll analyze your current site, identify areas for improvement, and deliver a modern, high-performing version that elevates your brand.

What technologies do you work with?

We work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Python, and more. For design, we use Figma and the Adobe Creative Suite. We choose the best tools for each project.
